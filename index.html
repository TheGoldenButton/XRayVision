Concept field

Attach one or more text files, or load the demo text, to build the field.
Desktop: drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, WASD/QE to fly, click a concept to select. VR: a query bar and keyboard float at the bottom of your view (point and pinch or trigger the bar to type). Pinch or trigger a concept to select, pinch empty space to drag, pinch with both hands and spread to zoom. Sticks fly (left move, right turn and height). A adds the selected concept to the query, B clears. AR (Quest 3): press Enter AR, look at a wall, table or floor, and pinch to place the field on that surface. Use Place on the panel to move it again.
