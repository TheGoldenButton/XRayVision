Gestures

  • Pinch & hold (one hand) – scan: the room image, your head pose and detected surfaces go to Muse Spark, which identifies objects and symbols and updates its own vector memory.
  • Pinch both hands & hold – ask a question aloud; release to send. Your speech is transcribed, Muse Spark answers with the room in view, and the best associative matches from memory float into view.
