Concept field

Attach one or more text files to build the field.
Desktop: drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, WASD/QE to fly, click a concept to select. VR: a query bar and keyboard float at the bottom of your view (point and pinch or trigger the bar to type). Mixed reality (Quest 3): press Enter mixed reality to see your room through the passthrough cameras. Pinch and drag anywhere to move the data sphere, pinch with both hands and spread to resize, quick pinch on a concept to select it. Sticks fly in VR. A adds the selected concept to the query, B clears. Press Place on the panel to snap the field onto a wall, table or floor.
