Concept field

Attach one or more text files, or load the demo text, to build the field.
Desktop: drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, WASD/QE to fly, click a concept to select. VR: pinch a concept to select, pinch empty space to drag, pinch with both hands and spread to zoom in or bring together to zoom out. Sticks fly (left move, right turn and height). A adds the selected concept to the query, B clears.
